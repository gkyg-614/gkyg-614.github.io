About

A little about my work

I'm a backend engineer who cares more about a system's failure modes than its happy path. Most of my career has been spent on services that move money or move data — places where a quiet bug on a Friday afternoon becomes everyone's Saturday.

Lately I've been deep in event-driven architecture and the tradeoffs between consistency and availability that never really go away, no matter how many queues you add. Outside of work I write about systems design and contribute to a couple of open-source tracing tools.
